# 1 The galaxy
40 planets, 10 per empire. Federation (Earth, bottom left), Romulan (Romulus, top left), Klingon (Klingus, top right), Orion (Orion, bottom right).
You win by conquest: an empire that loses its last planet is genocided, and when only one empire still holds planets, the galaxy is conquered and resets.
Kills are the currency. A kill is worth 1 + 0.1 x the victim's kills + 0.1 x the armies it carried. Kills let you carry armies, and armies take planets. Your kills reset when you die.

# 2 First flight
- Launch a cruiser (c): the best all-rounder.
- Cruise at warp 6. Top speed burns fuel and heats engines.
- Right-click or k steers toward the pointer. Head for the nearest border.
- Shields up (s) near enemies. Left-click fires a torpedo: lead your target.
- Phasers (p) hit instantly, out to 6,000 in a cruiser.
- Hurt? Orbit a friendly repair planet (o, or l to autopilot) and press R. You keep your kills if you live.
> Press ? for every key, L for players, P for planets, i for info on whatever is under the pointer.

@ DD Destroyer | Warp 10, 85/85. Raids, plasma

@ BB Battleship | Warp 8, 130/130. Holding a front

@ SB Starbase | Warp 2, 500/600. Fortress, one per team
Fast ships turn slowly: turn rate falls with the square of speed. Drop to warp 3-4 to turn inside an enemy. A damaged hull lowers your top speed.

# 5 The conquest loop
- Kill: each kill lets you carry 2 armies (3 in an assault ship).
- Bomb: orbit the target, b. Bombing stops at 4 armies.
- Load: orbit your own planet, z. One must stay behind.
- Invade: orbit the target, x. Each army kills a defender; the next takes it.
After bombing to 4 you need 5 armies. Neutral planets with no armies fall to one.
- Bomb first, carry second.
- Defenders carry over between ships: a teammate can beam 3, you finish with 2.
- A loaded carrier is the most valuable ship on the map. Hunt theirs, guard yours.
- Reinforce a new capture: it starts with 1 army.

# 6 Combat
- Torpedoes travel: aim where the target will be. D detonates yours early.
- Phasers hit the first enemy near the beam, instantly. Your finisher.
- Plasma homes in and hits hard. The only real weapon against Species 8472.
- d detonates incoming enemy torpedoes for 100 fuel.
- Below a quarter fuel, stop fighting and refuel.
- Overheated weapons lock up; overheated engines cut out.
- Fight near your planets: their guns shoot your pursuers.
- Exploding ships hurt everything within 3,000. Don't die next to friends.
- Tractor an enemy carrier into your planet's guns; pressor attackers off you.
> Hull past half and shields gone? Leave. A live cruiser with kills is a carrier in waiting.

# 7 Planets
- Planets start with 17 armies; home worlds with 30, plus repair, fuel and farming.
- Repair (R) fixes hull and shields; fuel (F) refuels; farming (A) grows armies faster.
- You only see an enemy planet's armies once your team has flown within 6,000.
- Planets fire on enemies within 1,500, harder with more armies. Shields up to bomb.
- Losing your last planet genocides your empire.

# 9 Careers
Points: kill credit, +0.5 per planet taken, +1 per order, +2 per honour.
@ Commander 15 | Starbases
@ Captain 25 | Weapons tech, special ship
@ Fleet Capt 40 | Defence tech
@ Commodore 60 | Tech on v, relic ships
@ Rear Adm 90 | Heavy weapon on e
@ Admiral 130 | Escape tech on j
From Captain up you're worth +0.5 kill per rank to whoever gets you. /tech lists your tech.
- Special ships (e): Defiant, D'deridex, Negh'Var, Corsair.
- Best relics (u): Voth city ship (4 armies/kill), Husnock (bombs to 1), Iconian (gateway jumps), Sheliak (unbombable planet).

# 10 The extras
## Terrain
- Nebulae and ion storms hide you beyond 3,000.
- Slipstreams add warp 3; wormholes cross the galaxy.
- Asteroids and planetoids soak up torpedoes: cover.
- Stars, comet tails and derelicts refuel and repair.
- Avoid the black hole, pulsar, minefields, Metreon gas and the galactic barrier.
## Supplies
Escort your freighter, raid theirs. /upgrade shields|repair|torps|phasers|engines (10, 20, 30 supplies).
## Orders
Each completed order is +1 kill and a full resupply. Take them.
## Subsystems
Hull hits can knock out a system; shields protect them. /fix warp to escape, /fix phasers to fight on, /fix transporters before a drop.
## Boarding
Shields down, within 1,500, armies aboard: B at the ship. Crew: SC 2, DD 3, CA 4, AS 5, BB 6. Double their number wins about 95%. Tow the prize home. Never board a Borg cube.
## Outposts
/build yard near the front first: you relaunch there. Defence on planets they keep bombing. Sensor arrays reveal cloaked ships within 12,000. 30 s in orbit; 15 supplies or 3 armies.
## Overwatch
In orbit, w fires at anything hostile in range, special weapons first. Off when you leave orbit.

# 12 The Ringworld
Ten sections around a planet, each a planet you can claim. Kzin (12 armies) is the Kzinti homeworld; three Kzinti ships always fly, relaunching a minute after loss.
Robots go into a ring frenzy and fight only for the ring.
> When the Kzinti lose their last section, the ring jumps away with every section on it, yours included.
- Grab unclaimed sections early.
- Don't sink your whole empire into the ring.
- Finishing the Kzinti strips rivals of their ring holdings.
